Who We Are

Redefining how
Muslims in Nigeria
access finance.

DOGO Finance is a digital Islamic asset management and financial services company created to redefine how Muslims in Nigeria access modern finance.

We exist to make halal wealth creation simple, transparent, and accessible through technology.

Our platform brings together Shariah-compliant investments, profit-generating wallets, digital payments, and global market access in one secure place — for everyday users and institutional partners alike.

We believe finance should be built on trust, clarity, and purpose. Every product we design reflects this belief.

We are building more than an application.

We are creating foundational infrastructure for Islamic finance in Africa.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver a regulated, technology-driven ecosystem that enables individuals and institutions to save, invest, transact, and grow wealth in full alignment with Shariah principles.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted digital Islamic finance platform, empowering millions with ethical wealth-building tools and seamless access to halal financial opportunities.

Why Now

Why we
exist now.

Nigeria is experiencing a rapid shift toward digital finance, driven by widespread mobile adoption, a youthful population, and growing demand for accessible financial services.

Islamic finance in Nigeria is gaining momentum through sukuk issuances, regulatory engagement, and rising awareness of Shariah-compliant products. Yet access to modern halal wealth tools remains limited and fragmented — leaving millions of Muslims underserved.

Globally, Islamic fintech is advancing rapidly across the Middle East and Southeast Asia, while Nigeria has yet to participate in this transformation fully.

Our Philosophy

We approach Islamic finance with discipline, structure, and long-term thinking — combining modern technology with Shariah governance to deliver practical wealth-building solutions.
